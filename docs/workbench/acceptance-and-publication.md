# Acceptance and publication

Workbench acceptance applies only to the agreed platform scope, validation
records, artifacts, findings, responsibilities, and decision. It does not prove
that every possible change or target is supported.

Public documentation under `https://docs.l-it.io/workbench/` requires:

- traceability of every material claim to the approved product authority;
- exclusion of customer, infrastructure, credential, secret, repository, and
  private-evidence details;
- exact-tree, role-authorized approval evidence;
- successful repository, preview, accessibility, search, link, and security
  checks;
- protected merge and deployment; and
- verification of the public route against the deployed commit.

The canonical product authority, **LIT-PRD-80-Workbench version 13**, authorizes
the route family and the initial
`https://docs.l-it.io/workbench/overview/` route. Source approval does not
replace repository or production controls.
