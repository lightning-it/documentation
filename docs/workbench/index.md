# Workbench overview

**Workbench** is Lightning IT's standardized engineering, development, and
validation platform for reproducible automation, Linux, container, and
integration testing, for internal use and customer environments. Its
conceptual verb is **Develop & Validate**.

Workbench is one of the
[five sellable products](../architecture/product-taxonomy-decision.md).
ModuLix is the reusable engineering and automation content foundation, not a
sixth product.

## Approved public outcomes

Workbench provides:

- reproducible development and test conditions;
- development, integration, and validation of changes;
- risk-based testing of automation, Linux, container, and integration
  scenarios;
- traceable test artifacts and acceptance evidence; and
- a bounded platform model for internal or customer use.

These statements do not promise a particular technology, version, integration,
repository structure, delivery package, compatibility level, service level, or
commercial term.
