# Compliance positioning

**Policy.** Framework mappings are navigation and assessment aids. They are not
legal advice, certification, regulatory approval, or proof of implementation.
The current canonical approach is the
[BSI mapping](../compliance/bsi-mapping.md).

**Target state.** Issue
[#138](https://github.com/lightning-it/documentation/issues/138) will introduce
framework-neutral mappings with explicit applicability, status, assurance,
evidence, exception, owner, and review fields. Until then, no portfolio-wide
compliance conclusion is published.
