# AI-assisted engineering

**Policy — engineering and documentation.** AI may assist discovery, drafting,
implementation, validation, and summarization. It may not approve a document,
accept risk, waive policy, determine classification alone, or authorize
production.

**Documented process.** A human owner selects scope and sources; deterministic
tools produce validation evidence; an authorized human reviews consequential
decisions and the exact candidate before promotion.

**Unverified gap — portfolio-wide measurement.** No public, independently
verified metric currently demonstrates how consistently this process is
executed across all products. Owner: AI Governance Owner. Review trigger:
publication of a safe, scoped measurement method and evidence.
